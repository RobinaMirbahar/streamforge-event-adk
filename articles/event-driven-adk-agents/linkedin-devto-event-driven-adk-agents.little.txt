Event-driven multi-agent pipelines on Google Cloud with ADK, Gemma 3 & Eventarc

Build a real-time event-driven multi-agent pipeline on Google Cloud using Eventarc, Cloud Pub/Sub, Cloud Run, open-weights Gemma 3, and Google Agent Development Kit \(ParallelAgent and LoopAgent\).

What is in it:

- 🟢 Concurrent Fan-Out \(ParallelAgent\): Extracted engineering specs \(73.8% latency drop from 145 ms to 38 ms at 250,000 rows/sec\), localized copy across 3 locales \(en\_US, de\_DE, ja\_JP\), and forecasted $2,856,000 in 12-month Net New ARR lift across 340 upgraded accounts \(18.5% of 1,840 active Growth Tier accounts at $8,400 annual expansion\).
- 🟢 Self-Correcting Quality Loop \(LoopAgent\): Detected the €459.08/mo and ¥75,848/mo currency formatting violations on Iteration 1 \(quality\_score = 0.72\), repaired them to €460/mo and ¥75,800/mo, and exited cleanly on Iteration 2 \(quality\_score = 1.0\) via exit\_quality\_loop\(\).
- 🟢 At-Least-Once Idempotency: Short-circuited duplicate ce-98104271-gcs-finalized redelivery in \< 5 ms with 0 redundant parallel branches or LLM calls.
